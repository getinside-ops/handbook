# Marketing that touches

::: tip In short
- **getinside** delivers your offers and samples right to consumers' homes, where 86% of purchase decisions are made.
- 3 use cases (Recruit, Retain, Monetise), 3 channels (parcel inserts, direct mail, sampling).
- 500+ brands already use it. Each brand stays in control of the offers shown to its customers.

## An all-in-one solution

- **AI & data targeting:** identify and reach your future customers.
- **Lookalike modelling:** custom modelling based on your customers.
- **Full automation:** launch and track your campaigns, or hand them over to our team.

## The brand stays in control

Each brand approves the brands and offers shown to its customers. We measure and optimise the performance of every campaign.

::: info Compliance
**GDPR** processing based on legitimate interest, with the right to object stated on every mailing. **AGEC law**: samples provided only at the consumer's prior request (Art. L541-15-10 of the French Environmental Code).
:::
