# How does it work?

::: tip In short
- Trusted brands deliver your offers in their parcels and mail. Each one approves what reaches its customers.
- AI targets affinity brands. Delivery revenue credits a Wallet that funds your own campaigns.
- Overview of use cases and key figures: [Discover getinside](/en/start-here/).
:::

| Actor | Action |
| :--- | :--- |
| 📦 **Brand that monetizes** | Sends parcels and mail to its customers and delivers campaigns in them |
| ⚡ **Platform** | Targets affinity audiences with AI |
| 📢 **Brand that recruits** | Has its offer or sample delivered to those audiences |
| 💰 **Wallet** | Receives revenue and funds the brand's campaigns |

## The transfer of trust

The consumer has already bought, trusts the brand that delivers, and opens the parcel, the mailbox, touches the sample. That trust and attention carry over to the offer delivered.

## Recruit: two modes

- **Media buy:** you have your offer or sample delivered to affinity brands' audiences, with a campaign budget.
- **Exchange:** no media buy, each brand delivers the other's offer in its parcels and keeps its customers.

AI targets affinity brands through **lookalike modelling**: a custom model based on your customers' transactional data, crossed with the history of campaigns already delivered.

## Monetize: the Wallet cycle

1. **Deliver** other brands' offers to your audience.
2. **Earn revenue**: your Wallet is credited automatically.
3. **Fund** your campaigns with that revenue, or withdraw it.

You can also answer brand requests, prospect other brands and run all your partners from a single solution.

## Retain

Your customers no longer open your emails, but they still open their mail and parcels:

- **Reactivation:** direct mail to email-inactive customers.
- **Parcel up-sell:** a dedicated offer inserted in every parcel delivered.
