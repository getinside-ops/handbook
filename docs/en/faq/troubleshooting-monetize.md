# Troubleshooting: Monetize

Wallet, withdrawal or campaign: find the answer in less than 2 minutes.

::: tip In short
- Balance at 0: declare your volumes (parcel inserts) or validate the broadcast (emailing), nothing is automatic.
- Balance "pending" or transfer missing: 30-day protection period, then an invoice is required.
- Can't honor a campaign: warn support before cancelling, penalties grow over time.
:::

## Wallet & payments

::: details "My available balance is 0 even though I distributed campaigns"
The Wallet is **not automatically** credited. In My Campaigns, open the campaign:
- 📦 **Parcel inserts:** Declare volume, every **Monday or Tuesday**.
- 📧 **Emailing:** **Validate broadcast** after sending.

Without this, funds remain **escrowed**.
:::

::: details "My balance is 'pending', not 'available'"
This is normal: after a confirmed distribution, earnings stay **pending** for 30 days (protection against anomalies), then become **available** for withdrawal. Past 30 days, contact support with the campaign number.
:::

::: details "I haven't received my transfer"
Check that:
- a **valid invoice** is attached to the request;
- the balance is **Available**;
- the IBAN is correct (Settings → Bank details);
- payment is issued **30 days end of month** after validation.

Otherwise, write to [support@getinside.fr](mailto:support@getinside.fr) with the request date, amount and your IBAN.
:::

:::: details "Funds were deducted from my Wallet without my request"
::: warning Automatic penalties
- Cancelling a validated campaign: penalty according to contract.
- No response within 48h to a proposal: penalty according to contract.
- Late response without valid reason: possible penalty.
:::

Each movement is detailed in **Wallet History**.
::::

:::: details "My funds have expired"
::: danger Expiration after 12 months of inactivity
With no login and no withdrawal for 12 consecutive months, available funds are definitively acquired by getinside (contractual rule).
:::

Log in at least once a quarter, or schedule an annual withdrawal. If you contest the expiration, contact support with your login history.
::::

## Campaigns & operations

::: details "I accepted a campaign but I can no longer honor it"
Contact your account manager or support **right away**, without cancelling from the platform. A postponement or a replacement by another brand is often possible. A last-minute cancellation costs more than an early one.
:::

::: details "I didn't answer a campaign proposal within 48h"
The 48h rule is contractual: the proposal **expires** and goes to another brand, with a **possible penalty** according to your contract. Enable email/SMS notifications in your Settings to avoid it.
:::

::: details "A campaign is incomplete or the received visual/brief is incorrect"
Do not start the distribution. Report the problem (campaign profile → Report a problem) or contact your manager: getinside contacts the brand whose campaign you deliver, and distribution is paused during the correction.
:::

## Data entry errors

::: details "I made a mistake entering my weekly volume"
If the system is locked, do not re-enter. Notify the brand through the campaign messaging, then write to [support@getinside.fr](mailto:support@getinside.fr) with the campaign number and the correct value: manual correction within 24h.
:::

::: details "I clicked 'Validate broadcast' too early or by mistake"
This action triggers invoicing and is normally irreversible. Notify getinside right away (chat or [support@getinside.fr](mailto:support@getinside.fr)): if no invoice has been issued, the action can be cancelled; otherwise a credit note is issued and the process restarted.
:::

## Account & affiliation

::: details "My referral rewards are not paid"
They are **automatically suspended** if you have unpaid invoices with getinside. Regularize them to unblock payments.
:::

::: details "My account is suspended or blocked"
Possible causes: unpaid invoices, non-compliance with platform rules, suspicious activity. Write to [support@getinside.fr](mailto:support@getinside.fr) with your account email and the displayed error message.
:::

## Can't find your answer?

Write to [support@getinside.fr](mailto:support@getinside.fr) or open a ticket (Menu → Support → New ticket), with the campaign ID, the amount in question and a screenshot if possible.
