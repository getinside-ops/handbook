# Account, Team & Audiences

::: details How do I add a new user?
In **My Organization → Team**, click `+ Add a member`, then enter the email and the role. Keep at least one backup member so you can answer campaign proposals within 48h.
:::

::: details How do I list a new audience?
In **Monetization → Catalog**, click `+ New audience` and fill in the profile: brand, persona, average basket.

→ [Audience Setup](/en/monetize/audience-setup)
:::

::: details Do I need to create multiple accounts if I have multiple sites?
No. One account hosts as many audience profiles as you have distinct sites or logistics flows. Each audience has its own formats and opening periods.
:::

::: details Is getinside present internationally?
Yes, throughout Europe. Add your European audiences to receive targeted campaigns for these countries from the same account.
:::

::: details How do I track my referrals (affiliation)?
In the **Affiliation** tab. You are rewarded on a recurring basis for their activity. Your earnings appear in **Transactions → Revenue** and can be withdrawn or reinvested from your Wallet.
:::

::: details How do I make my audience profile attractive to other brands?
Brands browse the catalog like a marketplace: your profile is your storefront.

- **Visuals:** photos of your packaging, examples of parcels, screenshots of your emails.
- **Precise persona:** age, product category, purchase frequency, geolocation.
- **Realistic Average Order Value (AOV):** a precise number is more reassuring than a vague range.
- **Several formats:** parcel inserts and emailing attract more than a single format.

→ [Audience Setup](/en/monetize/audience-setup)
:::
