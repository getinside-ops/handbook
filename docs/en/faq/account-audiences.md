# Account, Team & Audiences

::: details How do I add a new user?
Go to **My Organization → Team** and click `+ Add a member`. Enter the email address and the role. Always keep at least one backup member so you can answer campaign proposals within 48h.
:::

::: details How do I list a new audience?
Go to **Monetization → Catalog** and click `+ New audience`. Fill in the profile carefully: brand, persona, average basket. This information convinces brands looking for an audience.

→ [Detailed guide: Audience Setup](/en/monetize/audience-setup)
:::

::: details Do I need to create multiple accounts if I have multiple sites?
No. A single account hosts as many audience profiles as you have distinct sites or logistics flows. Each audience is independent, with its own available formats and opening periods.
:::

::: details Is getinside present internationally?
Yes, getinside operates throughout Europe. You can add your European audiences and receive targeted campaigns for these countries from the same account.
:::

::: details How do I track my referrals (affiliation)?
The status of your referrals is visible in the **Affiliation** tab. You are rewarded on a recurring basis for their activity. Your earnings appear in **Transactions → Revenue** and can be withdrawn or reinvested from your Wallet.
:::

::: details How do I make my audience profile attractive to other brands?
Brands that want to recruit browse the catalog like a marketplace. Your profile is your storefront. To receive more proposals:

- **Take care of the visuals:** photos of your packaging, examples of parcels, screenshots of your emails.
- **Describe your persona precisely:** age, product category, purchase frequency, geolocation.
- **Provide a realistic Average Order Value (AOV):** a precise number is more reassuring than a vague range.
- **Activate multiple formats:** an audience offering parcel inserts and emailing attracts more than a single-format one.

→ [Complete guide: Audience Setup](/en/monetize/audience-setup)
:::
