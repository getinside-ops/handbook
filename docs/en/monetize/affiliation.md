# Affiliate Program

Recommend getinside to the brands in your network and generate recurring passive income.

## Who to refer?

<div class="gi-value-grid">
  <div class="gi-value-card">

  <div class="gi-value-card">

</div>

## Your Compensation

::: tip
Your reward is credited to your **Wallet** with every invoice paid by the referral. It applies to every referred brand, whatever its use.
:::

:::tip Recurring income
Your reward is paid on each payment made by your referral — as long as they remain active on the platform.
:::
