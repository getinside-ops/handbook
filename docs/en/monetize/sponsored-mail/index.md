# Parcel inserts

::: tip In short
- You slip a partner brand's flyer into the parcels you already ship, with no change to your core logistics.
- **98% deliverability** and **100% attention**: everyone opens their parcels.
- You declare the distributed volumes every week, which triggers billing.
:::

Want to have your own flyer run instead? See the [Parcel Inserts guide for Recruit](/en/recruit/sponsored-mail/).

## The principle

The partner brand produces and prints its flyer. getinside delivers the materials ready to insert. Your teams add them during packing, in "On-Top" mode: over the contents, replacing nothing. The flyer is read during the unboxing, when the buyer is most attentive.

## The flow

1. **Reception** of the flyer pallets delivered by getinside.
2. **Insertion** into parcels following the "On-Top" rules and the defined exclusions.
3. **Weekly declaration** of the volumes distributed (Monday or Tuesday).
4. **Billing** triggered automatically from the declared volumes.

## Operational management

- [Logistics & Insertion](/en/monetize/sponsored-mail/logistics): pallet reception, "On-Top" rules, competitive exclusions.
- [Tracking & Reporting](/en/monetize/sponsored-mail/reporting): weekly declaration of volumes to trigger billing.

## What you keep control of

- **Just-in-time**: the algorithm takes into account your **Lead Time** and storage capacity. You only receive merchandise when you need it.
- **Guaranteed payment**: getinside secures the funds upfront. Once volumes are declared, your revenue is validated and credited to your Wallet within 30 days.
- **Control**: competitive exclusions and right of veto, no brand that conflicts with your business is inserted without your approval.
