# Parcel inserts

Slip a partner brand's flyer into the parcels you already ship. Every shipment becomes a paid space, with no change to your core logistics. **98% deliverability** and **100% attention**: everyone opens their parcels, and a channel that requires a physical action generates attention.

## The principle

The partner brand produces and prints its flyer. getinside delivers the materials ready to insert. Your teams add them to parcels during packing, in "On-Top" mode: over the contents, replacing nothing. You then declare the volumes distributed.

The flyer is read during the unboxing, when the buyer is most attentive. This explains the format's performance, and therefore its value to you.

## The flow

1. **Reception** of the flyer pallets delivered by getinside.
2. **Insertion** into parcels following the "On-Top" rules and the defined exclusions.
3. **Weekly declaration** of the volumes distributed (Monday or Tuesday).
4. **Billing** triggered automatically from the declared volumes.
