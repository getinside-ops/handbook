# Integration and Broadcast

Use the correct redirection link: it is what gets you paid.

<div class="gi-step">
  <div class="gi-step-num">1</div>
  <div class="gi-step-body">
    <h3>Receiving the visual</h3>
    <p>The brand sends you its banner (JPG, PNG or GIF, max 150 KB). Check that the format matches your placement (e.g. 600 × 150 px for a Leaderboard) and that it is readable on mobile.</p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="gi-step">
  <div class="gi-step-num">2</div>
  <div class="gi-step-body">
    <h3>Retrieving the tracked link</h3>
    <p>On <a href="https://app.getinside.media/" target="_blank">app.getinside.media</a>, in the campaign's <strong>"Configuration and tracking"</strong> tab, copy the <strong>tracked link</strong> (<code>https://gtinsi.de/…</code>). No open pixel for this format: only clicks are counted.</p>
  </div>
</div>

:::danger Tracked link mandatory
**Never** use the brand's raw link: without <code>gtinsi.de</code>, clicks are not counted and you are not paid.
:::

<div class="gi-step">
  <div class="gi-step-num">3</div>
  <div class="gi-step-body">
    <h3>Insertion into your router</h3>
    <p>In your emailing tool (Mailchimp, Brevo, Klaviyo...): insert the image, add a link to it, then paste the <code>gtinsi.de</code> link as the destination URL.</p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="gi-step">
  <div class="gi-step-num">4</div>
  <div class="gi-step-body">
    <h3>Proof test and validation</h3>
    <p>Send a test email to <code>benoit+news@getinside.fr</code>. Click <strong>"Validate the campaign"</strong> on the platform, then wait for the brand's validation.</p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="gi-step">
  <div class="gi-step-num done">✓</div>
  <div class="gi-step-body">
    <h3>Broadcast and final validation</h3>
    <p>Route the email to your base, then click <strong>"Validate the broadcast"</strong> on the platform.</p>
  </div>
</div>

::: warning Blocking action
Without **"Validate the broadcast"**, funds remain escrowed: this click triggers billing and the credit to your Wallet (available 30 days after the campaign ends).
:::
