# Dedicated Emailing

Turn your free sending slots into revenue by running dedicated campaigns for partner brands. It is the highest-earning format in the catalog: a single send, designed in your brand style, that recommends a brand to a segment of your base (endorsement).

You stay in charge of the editorial. You choose which brands speak to your subscribers, you build the email in your visual identity and you decide the target segment.

::: tip Want your campaign to be run by other brands?
Do you want to recruit through other brands' emailing? See the [Dedicated Email guide for Recruit](/en/recruit/dedicated-email/).
:::
