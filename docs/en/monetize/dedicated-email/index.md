# Dedicated Emailing

::: tip In short
- A single send, designed in your brand style, recommends a partner brand to a segment of your base (endorsement). It is the highest-earning format in the catalog.
- You choose the brands, build the email in your visual identity and decide the target segment.
- getinside provides the assets and tracking, and payment is guaranteed.
:::

Want to have your campaign run instead? See the [Dedicated Email guide for Recruit](/en/recruit/dedicated-email/).

## Your monetizable inventory

| Campaign | Principle | Strength |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Blast (Newsletter)** | Dedicated email to all or part of your active database | High volume |
| **Post-Purchase** | Dedicated email sent automatically after an order | Open rate > 50% |
| **Welcome Pack** | Presentation of a partner in the welcome sequence | Loyalty |

You validate each brand before sending.

## The steps

1. [Creation & Design](/en/monetize/dedicated-email/1-design): the brand's assets, native endorsement, examples.
2. [Exclusion lists](/en/monetize/dedicated-email/exclusion-list): set up your endpoint to apply the brand's exclusions before sending.
3. [Tracking & Sending](/en/monetize/dedicated-email/2-tracking-sending): open pixel, tracked gtinsi.de links, email proof and final validation.
