# Quick Start: Monetize

Did you receive the getinside invitation email? In 3 steps (10 minutes), your profile is ready to receive your first campaigns.

## Step 1: Activate your access (2 min)

1. Open the **invitation email** from getinside.
2. Click the activation link and set your password.
3. Log in at [app.getinside.media](https://app.getinside.media/).

Your account was created by the getinside team during your demo. No email? Check your spam folder or contact your manager.

## Step 2: Configure your logistics profile (5 min)

**Workspace:** Settings → Logistics. The platform only offers you campaigns compatible with these two pieces of information.

| Field | What to define |
|-------|----------------|
| **Warehouse address** | Where physical materials (boxes, flyers) will be delivered |
| **Lead Time** | Minimum delay between reception and start of distribution (e.g., 3 days) |

## Step 3: Create your first audience (3 min)

Your audience is what you offer to brands that want to reach it. The more precise it is, the more relevant campaigns you receive.

**Workspace:** Monetization → Create an audience

- **Audience name**: e.g. "Women's fashion e-commerce customers 25-45 yo"
- **Average Order Value** of your customers
- **Category** of your products
- **Weekly volume** of parcels (for Parcel Insert)

## Next

A **checkout call** with the getinside team confirms your listing on the marketplace. From then on, you receive **campaign proposals** on the platform.

::: warning 48-hour rule
You have **48 business hours** to respond to each proposal. Beyond that, it expires.
:::
