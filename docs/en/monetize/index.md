# Monetize

::: tip In short
- You deliver other brands' campaigns to your customers and generate revenue.
- Your revenue credits your Wallet and funds your acquisition. Records: 90% reinvested, €215K+ per year.
- You can also exchange your audiences with other brands, with no media buying.
:::

## What you can do

<div class="gi-value-grid">
  <div class="gi-value-card">
    <strong>Respond to brand briefs</strong>
    <p>Receive campaign proposals, set your terms, period, channels and estimated performance, and approve together.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="gi-value-card">
    <strong>Exchange your audiences</strong>
    <p>Agree on a visibility exchange with another brand, with no financial transaction.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="gi-value-card">
    <strong>Fund your acquisition</strong>
    <p>Your revenue credits your Wallet: reinvest it in your own campaigns or withdraw it.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="gi-value-card">
    <strong>Host your partner brands</strong>
    <p>Host your partner brands, the ones you already work with, and run everything from a single solution.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="gi-value-card">
    <strong>Prospect brands</strong>
    <p>Send your own proposals to brands that want to reach your audience.</p>
  </div>
