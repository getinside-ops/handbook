# Affiliate Program

Recommend getinside to your network (network brands or advertisers) and generate recurring passive income.

## Who to refer?

<div class="gi-value-grid">
  <div class="gi-value-card">
    <strong>📦 Other network brands</strong>
    <p>Do you know other sites that ship parcels or have an email database? Invite them to monetize their audience.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="gi-value-card">
    <strong>🛍️ Brands (Advertisers)</strong>
    <p>Invite your suppliers or partners to use getinside for their acquisition campaigns. Ideal for digitizing your Trade Marketing.</p>
  </div>
</div>

## Your Compensation

::: tip
Your reward is credited to your **Wallet** with every invoice paid by the referral. It applies to both Distributor and Advertiser referrals.
:::

:::tip Recurring income
Your reward is paid on each payment made by your referral — as long as they remain active on the platform.
:::
