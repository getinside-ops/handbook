<h2 id="a">A</h2>
<dl>
  <dt>Audience exchange</dt>
  <dd>Agreement between two brands: each carries the other's offer in its parcels, with no media spend, and keeps its own customers.</dd>
</dl>
<h2 id="b">B</h2>
<dl>
  <dt id="bat">BAT (Bon à Tirer) / Print Proof</dt>
  <dd>Final validation of the mockup before production or delivery. No changes afterwards.</dd>
  <dt>Bleed (Fond Perdu)</dt>
  <dd>Printed area 2-3 mm beyond the final format, to avoid white borders when cutting.</dd>
  <dt>Brand</dt>
  <dd>Company that uses getinside. A single brand can recruit, retain and monetize.</dd>
  <dt>Brief</dt>
  <dd>Document from the brand having its campaign delivered: objectives and graphic elements.</dd>
</dl>
<h2 id="c">C</h2>
<dl>
  <dt>Capping</dt>
  <dd>Volume limit on a campaign (e.g. 1 flyer maximum per parcel).</dd>
  <dt id="churner">Churner</dt>
  <dd>Customer who no longer opens your emails and no longer buys. Direct mail is used to win them back.</dd>
  <dt>Closed-loop attribution</dt>
  <dd>Links an exposure to a verified purchase using the brand's first-party data, through the QR code scan and the unique promo code, without third-party cookies. See <a href="/handbook/en/recruit/analytics">Tracking &amp; Measurement</a>.</dd>
  <dt>Co-branding</dt>
  <dd>Two brands associated in the same communication.</dd>
  <dt id="control-group">Control group</dt>
  <dd>Sample of customers set aside that does not receive the mailing. Comparing its purchases with the group that received it shows the real effect of the campaign.</dd>
  <dt>CPA (Cost Per Acquisition)</dt>
  <dd>Average cost to generate a sale or a lead.</dd>
  <dt>CPM (Cost Per Mille)</dt>
  <dd>Cost of 1,000 impressions (parcels shipped or emails received).</dd>
  <dt>CTR (Click-Through Rate / Scan rate)</dt>
  <dd>Share of recipients who click the email or scan the QR code.</dd>
</dl>
<h2 id="d">D</h2>
<dl>
  <dt>Direct mail</dt>
  <dd>Printed piece addressed to the customer by name, delivered to their mailbox.</dd>
  <dt>DNVB</dt>
  <dd>Digital Native Vertical Brand: a brand born on the internet that sells directly.</dd>
  <dt>Double tracking</dt>
  <dd>Compares platform statistics with real sessions measured through UTM on the brand's website (emailing, social ads).</dd>
  <dt>Dynamic QR Code</dt>
  <dd>2D code whose destination URL can be changed after printing. Generated by getinside in .svg format.</dd>
</dl>
<h2 id="e">E</h2>
<dl>
  <dt>Eco-design</dt>
  <dd>Reducing the environmental impact of printed materials. Plastic lamination is banned, for full recyclability.</dd>
  <dt>Endorsement</dt>
  <dd>Guarantee brought by the delivering brand to the brand it talks about.</dd>
  <dt>Exchange (Wallet)</dt>
  <dd>Reinvest Wallet revenue in your acquisition campaigns, or withdraw it.</dd>
  <dt>Expiry of the Wallet</dt>
  <dd>Funds not withdrawn are lost after 12 consecutive months of inactivity (no login, no movement).</dd>
</dl>
<h2 id="f">F</h2>
<dl>
  <dt>First party data</dt>
  <dd>Data the brand collects from its customers. getinside acts as a trusted third party: it never leaves the brand's secure environment.</dd>
  <dt>FOGRA39</dt>
  <dd>Standard colorimetric profile for offset printing in Europe.</dd>
</dl>
<h2 id="l">L</h2>
<dl>
  <dt>Lamination</dt>
  <dd>Plastic layer on paper, banned to keep flyers recyclable.</dd>
  <dt>Landing Page</dt>
  <dd>Destination page after interacting with the offer, the responsibility of the brand having its campaign delivered.</dd>
  <dt>Lead Time</dt>
  <dd>Incompressible logistics delay between receipt of materials at the warehouse and their insertion into parcels.</dd>
  <dt id="legitimate-interest">Legitimate interest</dt>
  <dd>GDPR basis that lets you write to a customer without prior consent, provided you inform them and give them a simple, free right to object.</dd>
  <dt>Lookalike Audience</dt>
  <dd>Targeting of profiles similar (purchasing behavior, demographics) to an existing customer base.</dd>
</dl>
<h2 id="m">M</h2>
<dl>
  <dt>Monetize</dt>
  <dd>Deliver other brands' campaigns in your parcels, to generate revenue that funds your acquisition.</dd>
</dl>
<h2 id="n">N</h2>
<dl>
  <dt>Network of brands</dt>
  <dd>Trusted brands that deliver partners' campaigns to their customers, each validating the brands and offers shown.</dd>
</dl>
<h2 id="o">O</h2>
<dl>
  <dt>Open Rate</dt>
  <dd>Share of recipients who opened the email.</dd>
  <dt>Opt-in</dt>
  <dd>Explicit consent to receive commercial communications. Mandatory for dedicated emails and newsletter placements.</dd>
</dl>
<h2 id="p">P</h2>
<dl>
  <dt>Parcel inserts (Insertion colis)</dt>
  <dd>Inserting an offer (flyer, card, sample) inside a partner brand's shipping parcel.</dd>
  <dt>Partnership Ads</dt>
  <dd>Meta format that sponsors a post published from a partner brand's account.</dd>
</dl>
<h2 id="q">Q</h2>
<dl>
  <dt>Quiet Zone (Zone Tranquille)</dt>
  <dd>3 mm inner safety margin, with no important text or logo.</dd>
</dl>
<h2 id="r">R</h2>
<dl>
  <dt>Recruit</dt>
  <dd>Have your campaign delivered by other brands to reach your future customers, with a media budget or through an exchange.</dd>
  <dt>Retain</dt>
  <dd>Reach your customers through direct mail, especially those who no longer open your emails, and trigger repurchase with an offer in every parcel.</dd>
  <dt>ROAS (Return On Ad Spend)</dt>
  <dd>Revenue generated for every euro spent on advertising.</dd>
  <dt>ROI</dt>
  <dd>Return on Investment.</dd>
</dl>
<h2 id="s">S</h2>
<dl>
  <dt>Sampling (Echantillonnage)</dt>
  <dd>Free sample to try a product, provided only at the consumer's prior request (AGEC law).</dd>
  <dt>SLA (Service Level Agreement)</dt>
  <dd>Responsiveness commitment: answer any proposal within 48 business hours.</dd>
  <dt>Spark Ads</dt>
  <dd>TikTok equivalent of Partnership Ads.</dd>
</dl>
<h2 id="t">T</h2>
<dl>
  <dt>Tracking Pixel</dt>
  <dd>1x1 image inserted in an email to count opens.</dd>
  <dt>Triman</dt>
  <dd>Mandatory logo in France indicating how to sort waste.</dd>
</dl>
<h2 id="u">U</h2>
<dl>
  <dt id="undelivered-mail">Undelivered mail</dt>
  <dd>Mail the postal service could not deliver (unknown address, moved). You receive a CSV file with the reason, to update your database.</dd>
  <dt>UTM</dt>
  <dd>Parameters added at the end of a URL (e.g. `?utm_source=getinside`) to track the traffic source.</dd>
</dl>
<h2 id="w">W</h2>
<dl>
  <dt>Wallet</dt>
  <dd>A brand's wallet, credited by the campaigns it delivers. Revenue funds its campaigns or can be withdrawn.</dd>
</dl>
