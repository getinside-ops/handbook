# Design Guide

At unboxing, your flyer has less than 3 seconds to convince.

::: tip In short
- Four steps: strategy, creative, legal, then a Proof (BAT) validated by getinside before any printing.
- Six mechanics, all with a QR code. The offer decides: monetary value, valid for at least 3 months, a unique promo code per delivering brand.
- Scanning happens on a smartphone: a responsive, fast and simple landing page.
:::

## Strategy & Mechanics

Choose the mechanic for your goal, then ask yourself: **"Why is this content meant for this audience?"** A fit with the delivering brand's universe makes a flyer kept rather than discarded.

| Mechanic | When to choose it | Example |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Promotion** | Immediate online conversion | "Scan for an exclusive offer" |
| **Drive to Store** | Physical traffic, or hybrid Web + store | "A free coffee in-store" |
| **Educational Content** | Brand image, long-term engagement | "5 seasonal recipes" |
| **Games** | Qualified leads (multi-format: [bundles](/en/recruit/bundles)) | "Scan to spin the wheel" |
| **Drive to App** | Mobile user acquisition | "Download the app, earn points" |
| **Feedback** | Customer insight, verified reviews | "Your opinion for a gift" |

## The Offer: what makes the difference

The offer is the most important decision: a good offer compensates for a mediocre layout. getinside is a **non-intentional medium**: only the strength of the offer removes the last hesitations.

| Parameter | Recommendation |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Type** | Monetary value ("€10 off") or a free product, rather than a percentage |
| **Validity** | 3 months minimum from printing: flyers are kept for weeks |
| **Promo code** | Unique per delivering brand, short, with its name ("BRAND20"), no `_`, `-`, `;` |

:::warning Price reductions: the reference price is mandatory
Any announced discount (percentage, struck-through price) requires showing the **lowest price applied in the previous 30 days** (Omnibus Directive, DGCCRF). A free monetary value or a gift isn't affected.
:::

## Mobile First

The post-scan experience is **99% on smartphone**. Your landing page must be ready before printing.

:::info 3 non-negotiable rules
1. **Responsive**: adapted to small screens.
2. **Fast**: immediate loading, even on 3G/4G.
3. **Simple**: one short form, a single call to action.
:::

## Copywriting & Paper UX

- **Hook:** the customer benefit first, not your brand.
- **QR code:** white background, black modules, 14 × 14 mm minimum, short instruction, bottom right ([specifications](./print-specs)).

## FAQ

::: details A brand refuses my visual: what to do?
The delivering brand has veto rights over the **brand** being inserted (direct competitor, incompatible category), not over art direction. If your file meets the specifications, it is compliant: send your proof to [studio@getinside.fr](https://lp.getinside.fr/meetings/benoit-prentout), getinside's validation prevails.
:::

::: details How do I track campaign performance?
Your getinside workspace shows QR scans in real time and inserted volumes (updated weekly). Add UTM parameters ([conventions](/en/recruit/analytics)) and a unique promo code per delivering brand.
:::
