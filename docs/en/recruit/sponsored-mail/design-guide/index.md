# Design Guide

Unboxing is an unusual moment. The consumer just received something they ordered, and they're attentive, hands busy, mind open. Your flyer has less than 3 seconds to make use of it.

From creative strategy to technical validation: here is how to create a flyer that converts.

::: tip In short
- Four steps: strategy, creative, legal, then a digital proof (BAT) submitted on the platform and validated by getinside before any printing.
- Six mechanics are available (promotion, drive to store, content, games, drive to app, feedback), all with a QR code as the entry point.
- The offer decides: a monetary value rather than a percentage, valid for at least 3 months, with a unique promo code for each brand that distributes the flyer.
- Scanning happens on a smartphone: the landing page must be responsive, fast and simple. The QR code is at least 14 × 14 mm.
:::

:::warning Mandatory validation
No content is delivered by another brand without prior validation from getinside via the platform. Submit your digital Proof (BAT) before any printing.
:::

## Strategy & Mechanics

Choose the mechanic adapted to your goal. All work with a QR code as the entry point. ## The Offer: what makes the difference

The offer is the most important decision in your flyer. A good offer compensates for a mediocre layout. The reverse is rarely true.

getinside is a **non-intentional medium**: when they discover your flyer, the consumer wasn't planning to buy from you. The strength of your offer removes the last hesitations. The more generous it is, the more it converts. You set the cursor.

:::tip Prioritize monetary value
**"10€ off"** converts much better than "-10% discount". Concrete value is immediately perceived. A free product or gift also works very well: the psychological pull of a gift outperforms a discount.
:::

:::info Validity period
Plan for a validity of **at least 3 months**. Recipients often keep flyers for several weeks before scanning them. An expired offer = a lost opportunity.
:::

| Parameter | Recommendation |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Type** | Monetary value > percentage |
| **Validity period** | Minimum 3 months from the print date |
| **Promo code** | Unique per delivering brand, short, no special characters (`_`, `-`, `;`) |
| **Promo code format** | Include the delivering brand's name, e.g. "BRAND20" |

:::warning Price reduction announcements: the reference price is mandatory
If your flyer announces a discount (percentage, struck-through price, "before/after"), French law requires you to show the **reference price**: the **lowest price applied in the 30 days** before the offer (Omnibus Directive, enforced by the DGCCRF). A free monetary value or a gift product isn't affected, which is one more reason to favor them.
:::

:::tip Protect your margin
Calibrate the offer to your margin and set a clear validity window (minimum 3 months). A generous but time-bound discount converts better than a permanent rebate that trains customers to wait.
:::

## Mobile First

The post-scan experience is **99% on smartphone**. Your landing page must be ready before printing.

:::info 3 non-negotiable rules
1. **Responsive**: the page must adapt perfectly to small screens.
2. **Fast**: loading must be immediate even on 3G/4G.
3. **Simple**: a single short form, a single call to action.
:::

## Copywriting & Paper UX

**Hook:** start with the customer benefit, not your brand name. "Your beauty routine, gifted in this parcel" > "Discover the new [Brand] collection".

**QR code** (see the [technical specifications](./print-specs) for print constraints):

- White background, black modules. Avoid textured backgrounds
- Minimum size: **14 × 14 mm**
- Short instruction next to it: "Scan to claim your offer"
- Position in the bottom right, where the thumb naturally holds a flyer

:::tip Go further
The message hierarchy, front/back structure, the 3-second test, the post-scan landing page, and the measurement loop are detailed in **[Anatomy of a High-Performing Flyer](./best-practices)**.
:::

## FAQ

::: details A brand refuses my visual: what to do?
The delivering brand has veto rights over the **brand** being inserted (direct competitor, incompatible category), but not over art direction. If your file meets the technical specifications, it is compliant. Send your proof to [studio@getinside.fr](https://lp.getinside.fr/meetings/benoit-prentout) for official validation: getinside's validation overrides the brand's subjective judgment.
:::

::: details How do I track campaign performance?
Your getinside workspace gives you access to QR scans in real time and inserted flyer volumes (updated each week). Add UTM parameters to your destination URL for Google Analytics tracking ([UTM conventions](/en/recruit/analytics)), and a unique promo code per delivering brand for offline attribution.
:::
