# Technical Specifications

The constraints to respect to guarantee optimal print quality and high-performance tracking.

---

## Paper Formats

| Format | Finished (cut) | File (with bleed) | Safe zone |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **A5** | 148 × 210 mm | **152 × 214 mm** | 3 mm internal |
| **A6** | 105 × 148 mm | **109 × 152 mm** | 3 mm internal |
| **Card** | 105 × 148 mm | **109 × 152 mm** | See template |

:::warning Bleed: 2 mm mandatory
The material (image or colored background) must extend **2 mm outside** the finished format. Without bleed, a white border appears after cutting.
:::

:::tip Safe zone: 3 mm
No text, logo, or important element should be less than **3 mm from the inner edge**. Anything too close to the edge risks being cropped.
:::

## Files & Colorimetry

| Criterion | Specification |
| :--- | :--- |
| **File format** | PDF/X vector (ISO prepress standard), preferred. JPG or PNG accepted. |
| **Resolution** | **300 DPI minimum** |
| **Raster dimensions** | A6: 1,287 × 1,795 px min · A5: 1,795 × 2,528 px min |
| **Color mode** | **CMYK mandatory** (no RGB) |
| **ICC Profile** | Coated FOGRA39 |
| **Ink coverage** | **Maximum 300%** |

:::tip Tip: deep black
For a rich black on solid areas (large black backgrounds): use **C30% M30% Y30% K100%**.
For fine texts and small characters: use **K100% only** to avoid shifting during printing.
:::

## Paper

:::info Recommended paper specifications
- **Weight:** 250 to 300 g/m²
- **Type:** Matte coated or semi-matte coated
- **Certification:** FSC or PEFC
- **Lamination:** no, lamination makes QR codes less readable
:::

:::info Mandatory environmental certification
**If getinside handles your printing:** the following notice must be printed on the flyer:
*"Imprimé sur papier FSC par un imprimeur certifié Imprim'vert avec getinside"* (Printed on FSC paper by an Imprim'vert-certified printer with getinside)
→ Include the FSC and Imprim'vert logos (in the [Mandatory logos](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1ORm6uAhsIWCCFWhOl-NjO4tKfiGQdLNy){target="_blank"} folder).

**If you manage your own printing:** only the **getinside logo** is required.
:::

→ The full CSR argument: [CSR & parcel inserts](/en/recruit/sustainability).

## getinside QR Code

:::danger Proprietary QR code mandatory
You **cannot** use a QR code generated by yourself. You must imperatively integrate the **`.svg`** file provided by the getinside platform.

**Why?** This unique QR code certifies distribution, enables real-time monitoring, and activates your performance dashboard.
:::

**Minimum size: 25 × 25 mm** (below this, the QR code is not readable by all smartphones).

**Do not mix QR codes:** each brand delivering your campaign has its own QR code. The `.svg` file is available in your workspace once the brand has approved your proposal.

## Proof (BAT) Checklist

To check before each submission:

1. Dimensions include **+2 mm bleed** and respect **-3 mm safe zone**.
2. File is in **CMYK (FOGRA39)**, resolution ≥ **300 DPI**, ink coverage ≤ **300%**.
3. getinside QR code (`.svg`) is present and measures at least **25 × 25 mm**. Each delivering brand has its own.
4. Universal legal notices are included: brand ID, Triman, getinside logo, T&Cs. If getinside handles printing, the FSC/Imprim'vert certification notice is present.
5. Mandatory [sector-specific notices](./legal) (if applicable) are present and legible.
6. The destination **landing page** has been tested on mobile: responsive, loads in < 3 s, a single call to action (see [Anatomy of a High-Performing Flyer](./best-practices#the-post-scan-landing-page)).
