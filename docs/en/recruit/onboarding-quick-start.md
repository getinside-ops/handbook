# Quick Start: Recruit

In 4 steps (15 minutes), your first campaign proposal goes to the brands that will deliver it.

::: tip In short
- Professional account (company ID required), then format choice and brief.
- The platform suggests brands, which accept, decline or counter-propose.
- **Validate the campaign within 48 business hours**: this is contractual, after that it may be canceled and penalties may apply.
:::

<div class="gi-step">
  <div class="gi-step-num">1</div>
  <div class="gi-step-body">
    <h3>Create your account (3 min)</h3>
    <p>On <a href="https://app.getinside.media/">app.getinside.media</a>, click <strong>"Create an account"</strong> and enter name, email, company ID and billing details, then validate your email. Invited by your account manager? Click the activation link in the invitation email.</p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="gi-step">
  <div class="gi-step-num">2</div>
  <div class="gi-step-body">
    <h3>Choose your format (2 min)</h3>
    <p>Not sure? Your account manager advises you during a 15-minute call.</p>
  </div>
</div>

| Format | Ideal Goal | Typical Timeline |
|--------|---------------|--------------|
| **Parcel Insert** | Physical acquisition, awareness, offline→online traffic | 2-4 weeks |
| **Dedicated Email** | Email acquisition, direct traffic | 1-2 weeks |
| **Newsletter Banner** | Visibility, remarketing | 1 week |
| **Social Ads** | Engagement, social media visibility | 1-2 weeks |
| **Bundle** | Qualified leads, integrated game | 2-3 weeks |

The Studio does not create the landing pages for games included in bundles: they stay the responsibility of the brand that delivers the game.

<div class="gi-step">
  <div class="gi-step-num">3</div>
  <div class="gi-step-body">
    <h3>Submit your brief (5 min)</h3>
    <p><strong>Workspace:</strong> My Campaigns → Create a campaign.</p>
  </div>
</div>

- **Basic information:** desired volume (parcels, contacts or impressions), budget, delivery dates, destination URL (with UTM if possible).
- **Creative material:** the promise of your offer (e.g. "-20% for new customers"), visuals following the [technical specifications](/en/recruit/sponsored-mail/design-guide/print-specs), key arguments. No visuals? The **getinside Studio** creates your assets.

A side panel shows real-time reach estimates (contact volume, estimated CTR and CPC) and your budget breakdown (audience, Studio, logistics).

::: details Trust Index
A score from 1 to 5 based on your shared history with the delivering brand: 1 unlikely, 4 guaranteed, 5 maximum confidence.
:::

<div class="gi-step">
  <div class="gi-step-num done">4</div>
  <div class="gi-step-body">
    <h3>Choose the brands and validate (5 min)</h3>
    <p>View the suggested profiles (sector, average basket, weekly volume). A bar shows your acceptance likelihood in real time. Brands can <strong>accept, decline or counter-propose</strong>.</p>
  </div>
</div>

Once agreement is reached, **validate the campaign within 48 business hours**: a Purchase Order is automatically generated. Then validate the **Proof (BAT)**, the final visual and technical check before production.

Track performance in **My Campaigns → Statistics**.

**Next steps:** [Parcel Insert Guide](/en/recruit/sponsored-mail/) (technical and creative specs) and [Creative Studio](/en/resources/studio) (delegate creation).
