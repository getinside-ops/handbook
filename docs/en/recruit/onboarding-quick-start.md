# Quick Start: Recruit

You want to launch your first campaign on getinside. In 4 steps (15 minutes), your campaign proposal goes to the brands that will deliver it.

::: tip
**This guide is for you if:** you have a getinside account (or are creating one) and want to launch your first campaign quickly.
:::

---

## Step 1: Create your account (3 min)

Access is **reserved for professionals** (company ID required).

1. Go to [app.getinside.media](https://app.getinside.media/)
2. Click on **"Create an account"**
3. Enter your information: name, email, company ID, billing details
4. Validate your email: your account is activated

> If you were invited by your account manager, open the invitation email and click the activation link.

---

## Step 2: Choose your format (2 min)

Each format targets a different goal:

| Format | Ideal Goal | Typical Timeline |
|--------|---------------|--------------|
| **📦 Parcel Insert** | Physical acquisition, awareness, offline→online traffic | 2-4 weeks |
| **📧 Dedicated Email** | Email acquisition, direct traffic | 1-2 weeks |
| **📩 Newsletter Banner** | Visibility, remarketing | 1 week |
| **📱 Social Ads** | Engagement, social media visibility | 1-2 weeks |
| **🎁 Bundle** | Qualified lead collection, integrated game | 2-3 weeks |

The Studio does not create the landing pages for games included in bundles: they stay the responsibility of the brand that delivers the game.

> Not sure? Your getinside account manager advises you based on your goals during a 15-minute call.

---

## Step 3: Submit your brief (5 min)

**Workspace:** My Campaigns → Create a campaign

You will need:

**Basic information:**
- Desired volume (number of parcels, contacts, impressions depending on format)
- Budget and delivery dates
- Destination URL (with UTM if possible)

**Creative material:**
- The promise of your offer (e.g., "-20% for new customers")
- Your visuals (according to [technical specifications](/en/recruit/sponsored-mail/design-guide/print-specs))
- Your key arguments

While you configure the campaign, a side panel shows real-time reach estimates (contact volume, estimated CTR and CPC) and your budget breakdown (audience, Studio, logistics).

Also check the **Trust Index**: a score from 1 to 5 based on your shared history with the delivering brand. 1 means unlikely, 4 guaranteed, 5 maximum confidence.

> No visuals? The **getinside Studio** creates your creative assets. Contact your manager to learn more.

---

## Step 4: Choose the brands delivering your campaign and validate (5 min)

1. The platform offers you a **selection of brands** whose audience matches your criteria
2. View profiles: sector, average basket, weekly volume
3. A visual bar shows your acceptance likelihood in real time as you adjust your proposal
4. Brands receive your proposal and can **accept, decline or counter-propose**
5. Once agreement is reached, **validate the campaign within 48 business hours**: a Purchase Order is automatically generated
6. Validate the **Proof (BAT)**: the final visual and technical check before production

---

## Your campaign is launched ✅

Track performance in real time in **My Campaigns → Statistics**.

**Important reminder:** validation within 48h is contractual. After this period, the campaign may be canceled and penalties may apply.
