## How does it work?

Rather than running a classic ad from your account, you sponsor a post published from the partner brand's account (**Partnership Ads** on Meta, **Spark Ads** on TikTok). The brand adds the mandatory *"Paid partnership"* mention.

The difference from standard social advertising: you don't appear as an unknown brand in a feed. A brand the consumer already knows and follows introduces you. Your message inherits the trust that brand has built with its community.

**The 4-step workflow:**

1. **Brief and selection**: choose the partner brand and send the creative angle, visuals, key messages and destination URL.
2. **Creation**: you provide the ready-to-post content, or the brand or the getinside Studio creates it for a native feel. getinside coordinates creative validation with the partner brand, and you receive a preview before delivery.
3. **Publication**: the brand publishes with the *"Paid partnership"* mention and activates the amplification rights, on the agreed dates.
4. **Amplification**: getinside activates the Ads campaign on the post to reach a wider audience (followers + Lookalike).

## Formats and specifications

| Format | Dimensions | Recommendation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Video** (Reels/TikTok) | 9:16, 1080 × 1920 px, 15 to 60 s | Recommended: 15% safe zone at top and bottom |
| **Feed image** | 4:5, 1080 × 1350 px | Keep image text light: Meta recommends under 20% of the surface (better performance, no delivery penalty) |
| **Story** | 9:16, 1080 × 1920 px, 15 s/slide | Leave space for the link sticker |
