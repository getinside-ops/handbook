# Social Ads (Partnership Ads)

Run your ads via the Instagram, TikTok and Facebook accounts of our partner brands, drawing on their credibility with their community.

::: tip In short
- You sponsor a post published from the partner brand's account (**Partnership Ads** on Meta, **Spark Ads** on TikTok), with the mandatory *"Paid partnership"* mention.
- A brand the consumer already knows introduces you: your message inherits its trust.
- Uses: awareness (qualified audience, no cold targeting), drive-to-site, app install.
:::

:::info Format on demand
This format is available by contacting our team: [book a meeting with the studio](https://lp.getinside.fr/meetings/benoit-prentout)
:::

## Formats and specifications

| Format | Dimensions | Recommendation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Video** (Reels/TikTok) | 9:16, 1080 × 1920 px, 15 to 60 s | 15% safe zone at top and bottom |
| **Feed image** | 4:5, 1080 × 1350 px | Light text: Meta recommends under 20% of the surface (better performance, no delivery penalty) |
| **Story** | 9:16, 1080 × 1920 px, 15 s/slide | Space for the link sticker |
