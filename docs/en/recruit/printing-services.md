# Printing & Logistics

getinside acts as a **central purchasing office**: pooled volumes, lower costs, responsible production.

::: tip In short
- 100% French production, certified (Imprim'Vert, FSC/PEFC, ISO 9001 and 14001).
- A single item: printing, finishing and transport, delivered to the warehouse of the brand delivering your campaign.
- Manual assembly entrusted to Adapted Enterprises.
:::

:::info Need more than a printer?
The [getinside agency](https://www.getinside.fr/agence){target="_blank"} supports you beyond printing: a dedicated contact, the Studio to design your materials, and advice to optimize your campaigns.
:::

## Certified quality

- **Made in France:** 100% of production in French workshops.
- **Eco-responsibility:** Imprim'Vert, vegetable inks, FSC and PEFC paper.
- **ISO standards:** ISO 9001 (Quality) and ISO 14001 (Environment).

## Paper specifications

| Criterion | Specification |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Formats** | A5 (148 × 210 mm), A6 (105 × 148 mm), Gift card |
| **Weight** | 250 to 300 g/m² |
| **Type** | Matte coated or semi-matte coated |
| **Paper certification** | FSC or PEFC mandatory |
| **Lamination** | No: makes QR codes less readable |

## Use cases

- **Your campaigns (Parcel inserts):** getinside prints your materials, manages assembly (e.g., fugitive glue dot for a sample) and delivers directly to the partner brand's warehouse.
- **Self-promotion:** thank-you cards or flyers for **your own parcels**. Create an "internal" campaign on the platform and benefit from group purchasing. Pallet delivery to your door.

The cost is displayed during campaign configuration and decreases per unit as volume grows.

:::tip Just-in-time delivery
Prints reach the brand's warehouse synchronized to its logistics **Lead Time**: zero unnecessary storage on your end.
:::

## Disability commitment

For all manual operations (assembly, gluing samples, enveloping), getinside works exclusively with **Adapted Enterprises**, which employ at least **80% workers with disabilities** in their production staff. You support the professional integration of people distanced from the labor market.

:::tip A brand hesitating for CSR reasons?
Parcel inserts hold up against a CSR policy: certified paper, no dedicated mailing, targeted distribution with no wasted print run. → [See the CSR & parcel inserts page](./sustainability)
:::
