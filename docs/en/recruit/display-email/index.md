# Newsletter Insert (Display Email)

Insert a banner within a partner's communications: you only provide an image and a link.

::: tip In short
- An image (JPG/PNG/GIF) and a link are enough: no HTML, no elaborate creative brief.
- Live 48 hours after you submit the banner.
- Closed, brand-safe environment endorsed by the brand delivering your campaign.
:::

::: tip Do you deliver other brands' campaigns to your customers?
See the [Newsletter Insert guide on the Monetize side](/en/monetize/display-email/).
:::

## Where to broadcast your banner

| Placement | Principle | Open rate |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Blast (editorial newsletter)** | Partner's weekly newsletter, in the header or core content | Variable (database and sector) |
| **Post-Purchase (transac)** | Order or delivery confirmation email, most premium placement | > 60% |
| **Welcome Email** | Welcome email for new subscribers, moment of maximum attention | High |

## Use cases

- **Flash or seasonal offer:** Black Friday, sales, holidays, with no print constraints.
- **Product launch:** introduce something new to a qualified audience.
- **Test campaign:** test a message or an audience before a heavier format.

:::info Tracking: clicks only
There is **no open pixel**: only clicks are counted (tracked link `gtinsi.de`). Add UTM parameters to your final URL to measure traffic in Google Analytics.
:::
