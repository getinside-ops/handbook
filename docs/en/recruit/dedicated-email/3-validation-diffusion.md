# Step 3: Validation and Broadcast

Creation is complete. Validate the proof (BAT) to authorize sending.

<div class="gi-step">
  <div class="gi-step-num">1</div>
  <div class="gi-step-body">
    <h3>Tracking configuration</h3>
    <p>getinside and the brand delivering your campaign embed the open pixels and tracked links. Your links are dynamic: you can change the destination URL at any time in the <strong>"Configuration and Tracking"</strong> tab, even after validation.</p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="gi-step">
  <div class="gi-step-num">2</div>
  <div class="gi-step-body">
    <h3>Receiving the proof (BAT)</h3>
    <p>Once the brand has finalized the email and getinside has technically verified it, you receive an email notification. A <strong>static preview</strong> lets you check adherence to the brief (offer, visuals, wording).</p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="gi-step">
  <div class="gi-step-num">3</div>
  <div class="gi-step-body">
    <h3>Your validation</h3>
    <p>If the creative suits you, validate it on the platform to unblock sending.</p>
  </div>
</div>

:::warning Required action
1. Log in to [app.getinside.media](https://app.getinside.media/){target="_blank"}.
2. Open the relevant campaign.
3. Click **"Validate campaign"**.

Until you validate, the campaign is not broadcast.
:::

<div class="gi-step">
  <div class="gi-step-num done">4</div>
  <div class="gi-step-body">
    <h3>Broadcast</h3>
    <p>Once your validation and the brand's are recorded, the campaign is scheduled and sent to the targeted audience. Track opens and clicks in real time from your dashboard.</p>
  </div>
</div>

::: info Delay between validation and sending
Expect 24 to 48 business hours: the brand schedules the campaign in a slot consistent with its own emailing activity. Plan for it if your offer is tied to a specific date.
:::

::: details Common reasons for proof corrections
- **Inconsistent offer**: amount, threshold or promo code that differs from the brief.
- **Low-resolution visual**: pixelated packshot or blurry logo.
- **Wrong link**: broken URL, or unrelated to the offer.
- **Missing legal notices**: obligations specific to the sector (alcohol, credit, gambling, etc.).
:::

:::tip Resend to non-openers
Some brands automatically resend the campaign to subscribers who did not open the first send. Ask for it during the brief.
:::
