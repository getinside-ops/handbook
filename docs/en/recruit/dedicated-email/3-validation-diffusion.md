# Step 3: Validation and Broadcast

Creation is complete. Your action is required: validate the proof (BAT) to authorize sending.

---

<div class="gi-step">
  <div class="gi-step-num">1</div>
  <div class="gi-step-body">
    <h3>Tracking configuration</h3>
    <p>This step is invisible to you. The getinside team and the brand delivering your campaign embed the open pixels and tracked redirection links.</p>
    <p><strong>Reminder:</strong> your links are dynamic: you can change the destination URL at any time in the <strong>"Configuration and Tracking"</strong> tab, even after validation.</p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="gi-step">
  <div class="gi-step-num">2</div>
  <div class="gi-step-body">
    <h3>Receiving the proof (BAT)</h3>
    <p>As soon as the brand has finalized the email and getinside has technically verified it, you receive an email notification.</p>
    <p>You can access a <strong>static preview</strong> of the campaign to check adherence to the brief (offer, visuals, wording).</p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="gi-step">
  <div class="gi-step-num">3</div>
  <div class="gi-step-body">
    <h3>Your validation: action required</h3>
    <p>If the creative suits you, validate it on the platform to unblock sending.</p>
  </div>
</div>

:::warning Required action to unblock sending
1. Log in to [app.getinside.media](https://app.getinside.media/){target="_blank"}.
2. Open the relevant campaign.
3. Click the **"Validate campaign"** button.

Until you validate, the campaign remains blocked and is not broadcast.
:::

<div class="gi-step">
  <div class="gi-step-num done">4</div>
  <div class="gi-step-body">
    <h3>Broadcast</h3>
    <p>Once both validations are recorded (yours and the brand's), the campaign is scheduled and sent to the targeted audience.</p>
    <p>Track open and click statistics in real time from your dashboard.</p>
  </div>
</div>

::: info Delay between validation and sending
Expect 24 to 48 business hours between your validation and the actual send: the brand schedules the campaign in a slot consistent with its own emailing activity. Plan for this delay if your offer is tied to a specific date.
:::

## Common reasons for corrections

Before reaching you, the proof may go back and forth with the brand. The most common reasons:

- **Inconsistent offer**: amount, threshold or promo code that does not match the brief.
- **Low-resolution visual**: pixelated packshot or blurry logo on display.
- **Wrong destination link**: broken URL, or one pointing to a page unrelated to the offer.
- **Missing legal notices**: obligations specific to the sector (alcohol, credit, gambling, etc.).

:::tip Resend to non-openers
Some brands offer to automatically resend the campaign to subscribers who did not open the email during the first send. Ask for it during the brief to maximize coverage.
:::
