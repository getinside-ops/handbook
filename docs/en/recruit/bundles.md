# Bundles & Media Kits

A Bundle is a coordinated campaign that activates several inventories from a single partner brand in one operation: parcel inserts, email, social, display.

::: tip In short
- A Bundle creates a presence effect that improves conversion.
- You provide visuals, prize and promo code, the partner brand handles the landing page.
- Only consenting participants are passed on to you.
:::

::: tip Do you deliver other brands' campaigns to your customers?
Want to package your inventory into Bundles? See the [Bundles guide on the Monetize side](/en/monetize/bundles).
:::

## How it works

1. **Choose the partner brand and mechanic.** Look for the "Multi-channel Offers" icon in the catalogue, then pick the mechanic based on your objective.
2. **Provide your assets.** Via the platform: visuals (HD logo, packshots), offer or prize, consolation promo code. The partner brand creates the co-branded landing page on its domain.
3. **The campaign runs across all channels** in parallel: flyers in parcels, dedicated newsletter, homepage banners, Instagram and TikTok posts.
4. **Collect leads.** At close, the partner brand sends the secure CSV of opt-ins via getinside. Import it into your CRM (Mailchimp, Klaviyo…) and send the welcome email with the promised promo code **immediately**.
5. **Contest:** you draw the winners of the main prizes and handle their shipping.

## Performance

- **Qualified opt-in leads:** no losers, consolation promo code for everyone.
- **Stronger brand recall:** three exposures (physical, email, social).
- **Strong engagement:** you benefit from the partner brand's credibility.

::: tip Field example
AJY (wellness brand) achieved a 21.6% scan rate with a "Discover your Dosha" quiz, one of the highest scores on the network.
:::

::: details Available mechanics
| Mechanic | Primary objective |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Promotion** | Direct conversion via promo code or discount voucher |
| **Contest** (Golden Ticket, Fortune Wheel, Quiz) | Opt-in leads, with a consolation promo code for everyone |
| **Content / Brand Content** | Awareness and expert positioning |
| **Drive to Store** | Foot traffic to physical locations |
| **Drive to App** | Qualified app installs |
| **Feedback / Rating** | Review collection and consumer panels |
:::

::: details GDPR framework for contests
At sign-up, the participant ticks an explicit box: "☑ I agree to receive commercial offers from the partner [your brand name]." Only participants who ticked it are passed on to you.
:::

::: details What you provide
- **The prize:** the hook product (e.g. *"Win a year of products"*), shipped by you.
- **The consolation promo code:** generic (e.g. `MERCI15`), offered to all.

The Bundle (channel, volume) can be adjusted with the partner brand.
:::
