# Bundles & Media Kits

A Bundle is a coordinated campaign that activates several inventories from a single partner brand in one operation: parcel inserts, email, social, display. A standalone campaign creates one exposure. A Bundle creates a presence effect that improves conversion.

::: tip Do you deliver other brands' campaigns to your customers?
Want to package your inventory into Bundles? See the [Bundles guide on the Monetize side](/en/monetize/bundles).
:::

## Available mechanics

| Mechanic | Primary objective |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Promotion** | Direct conversion via promo code or discount voucher |
| **Contest** (Golden Ticket, Fortune Wheel, Quiz) | Opt-in leads, with a consolation promo code for everyone |
| **Content / Brand Content** | Awareness and expert positioning |
| **Drive to Store** | Foot traffic to physical locations |
| **Drive to App** | Qualified app installs |
| **Feedback / Rating** | Review collection and consumer panels |

## GDPR framework for contests

At sign-up, the participant ticks an explicit box:

> ☑ I agree to receive commercial offers from the partner [your brand name].

Only participants who ticked this box are passed on to you.

## What you provide

- **The prize**: the hook product that attracts players (for example *"Win a year of products"*). You handle shipping to the winners after the draw.
- **The consolation promo code**: a generic code (for example `MERCI15`) offered to all participants.

You can adjust the contents of the Bundle (remove a channel, change volume) in direct discussion with the partner brand.
