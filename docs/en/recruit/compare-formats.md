# Choosing Your Format

::: tip In short
- Parcel insert: acquisition at scale, 2 to 4 weeks.
- Dedicated email, Social Ads: awareness and engagement, 1 to 2 weeks.
- Newsletter banner: fast launch, 48h.
:::

## Comparison

| Format | Lead time | Creative effort | Main goal | Details |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Parcel insert** | 2 to 4 weeks | High (print-ready PDF) | Acquisition, conversion | [See](/en/recruit/sponsored-mail/) |
| **Dedicated email** | 1 to 2 weeks | Medium (HTML or creative brief) | Awareness, drive-to-site, engagement | [See](/en/recruit/dedicated-email/) |
| **Newsletter banner** | 48h | Low (one image is enough) | Seasonal, fast launch | [See](/en/recruit/display-email/) |
| **Social Ads** | 1 to 2 weeks | Medium (brief + existing visual) | Awareness, app install | [See](/en/recruit/sponsored-social/) |
| **Bundle** | 2 to 3 weeks | High (one scenario, several media) | Lead collection, presence effect | [See](/en/recruit/bundles) |

## Which format for which goal?

- **Recruit at scale: parcel insert.** A physical flyer in a parcel, 100% attention at unboxing, a measurable offer (promo code, gift card, QR code).
- **Tell your story: dedicated email.** A full email signed by the delivering brand: several visuals, long copy, several CTAs. For launches and complex campaigns.
- **Move fast: newsletter banner.** A banner and a link, no print production. For Black Friday, sales or any short-notice launch.
- **Build awareness: Social Ads.** Your message carried by the delivering brand's social account (Instagram, TikTok, Facebook). Available on request.
- **Make an impression: bundle.** Several formats at one brand around a single scenario, with a built-in game to collect leads.
