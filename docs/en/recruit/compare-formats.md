# Choosing Your Format

Five formats, five ways to reach an audience. Here is how to choose based on your objective and timeline.

## Quick comparison

| Format | Type | Time to live | Creative effort | Primary objective |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Parcel Insert** | Physical print | 2 to 4 weeks | High (print-ready PDF) | Acquisition, conversion |
| **Dedicated Email** | Full email | 1 to 2 weeks | Medium (HTML or creative brief) | Awareness, drive-to-site, engagement |
| **Newsletter Banner** | Newsletter banner | 48 hours | Low (one image) | Seasonal, fast launch |
| **Social Ads** | Boosted post | 1 to 2 weeks | Medium (brief + existing visual) | Awareness, app install |
| **Bundle** | Multi-format package | 2 to 3 weeks | High (one scenario, several materials) | Lead collection, presence effect |

## Which format for which objective?

### Acquisition at scale: Parcel Insert

The parcel insert is the most tangible format: a physical flyer inside a parcel. It captures 100% of attention during unboxing and carries a concrete offer (promo code, gift card, QR code). Ideal for recruiting new customers with a measurable offer mechanic.

[Learn more about parcel inserts →](/en/recruit/sponsored-mail/)

### Engagement and storytelling: Dedicated Email

The dedicated email gives you the space of an entire email to tell your story: multiple visuals, long copy, multiple CTAs. The delivering brand signs the email in its own name. You benefit from its customer relationship. Ideal for launches, complex operations, or premium audiences.

[Learn more about dedicated email →](/en/recruit/dedicated-email/)

### Fast and seasonal campaigns: Newsletter Banner

The newsletter banner goes live in 48 hours. One banner, one link, a qualified audience in their inbox. No print production, no lead time. Ideal for Black Friday, sales, or any short-deadline launch.

[Learn more about the newsletter banner →](/en/recruit/display-email/)

### Social reach via trusted accounts: Social Ads

Social Ads uses the social account of the delivering brand (Instagram, TikTok, Facebook) to carry your message. You benefit from its audience and credibility, without appearing as a cold ad. Available on request.

[Learn more about Social Ads →](/en/recruit/sponsored-social/)

### Presence effect and qualified leads: Bundle

A bundle combines several formats with the same brand (parcel insert, email, social) around a single scenario, with an integrated game if you want to collect leads. Ideal for an operation that has to stand out.

[Learn more about bundles →](/en/recruit/bundles)
