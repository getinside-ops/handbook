# CSR & parcel inserts: responsible paper advertising

Parcel inserts rely on a paper material, and that is often what makes a CSR team hesitate. The question is fair: print has an impact. But it is worth looking at how the insert is produced and who receives it, rather than treating it like a mass-distributed leaflet.

This page gathers the concrete arguments, for getinside teams as much as for brands that want to check parcel inserts against their CSR commitments.

## Why parcel inserts hold up against a CSR policy

- **No dedicated mailing.** The insert goes into a parcel that is already packed and shipping: no extra envelope, no additional delivery round. The transport impact stays marginal, whereas an addressed mailing or an unaddressed leaflet is routed for itself alone.
- **No wasted print run.** The material only goes to customers who receive a parcel: they all have it in hand, while a mailbox leaflet often ends up in the bin unread. Volumes are set against retailers' distribution forecasts.
- **FSC or PEFC certified paper**, printed with vegetable inks. Workshops are Imprim'Vert and ISO 14001 certified.
- **Printed in France.** All production takes place in French workshops: a short supply chain, limited transport, no offshore printing.
- **A social and environmental impact.** Manual assembly is carried out by Adapted Enterprises, which employ at least 80% workers with disabilities.

## A recyclable material

When getinside handles printing, the paper is guaranteed free of plastic lamination: it is a single-material graphic paper that sorts like any other paper. The Triman and Info-tri signage appears on every visual, as the AGEC law requires, so the recipient knows what to do with it.

::: details What FSC, PEFC, Imprim'Vert and ISO mean
- **FSC and PEFC.** Two international standards certifying that the paper comes from sustainably managed forests: replanting, biodiversity protection, respect for local communities.
- **Imprim'Vert.** A French label confirming that the printer manages its hazardous waste, avoids the most toxic products, and secures its storage.
- **ISO 14001 and ISO 9001.** International standards for environmental management (14001) and quality management (9001) applied to the workshop.
- **Vegetable inks.** Inks based on vegetable oils rather than mineral ones (petroleum-derived), which are easier to de-ink when recycling.
:::

## Reduce your campaign's impact even further

A few levers you keep in hand:

- **Format.** A 10 × 15 cm card or an A6 uses less paper than an A5, for the same message.
- **Recycled paper.** Available on quote if your CSR specifications call for it.
- **Dynamic QR code.** Its destination URL can be changed after printing: one material can serve several periods without a reprint.
- **Volumes.** Set the ordered quantity as close as possible to the distribution forecasts to limit overproduction.
