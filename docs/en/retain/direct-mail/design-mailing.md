# Design your direct mail

The getinside studio creates your mailing by default, in one or several versions. This page explains what your brief contains, and how to create your own visual.

## Hand the creative to the studio

Send the studio a brief: the offer, the promo code, the target and your brand elements (logo, colors, fonts). It produces the visual or visuals, sets up the personalized fields and prepares the proof for each version. It also sets up the mailing template your file is linked to through a unique operation code. Write to [studio@getinside.fr](mailto:studio@getinside.fr).

## Create it yourself

### The format

- **Format:** A5, 148 × 210 mm, 250 g/m² paper, in a plain white envelope with no window. The address is printed on the envelope.
- **Paper, colors, QR code:** see the [print specs](/en/recruit/sponsored-mail/design-guide/print-specs).
- **Message and layout:** see the [anatomy of a high-performing flyer](/en/recruit/sponsored-mail/design-guide/best-practices).

### Unique QR code and promo code

These two options give tracking by customer. They are not mandatory.

- **Unique QR code:** each recipient gets their own QR code. getinside creates the list of unique URLs. You do not generate the QR codes yourself.
- **Unique promo code:** you create one code per recipient in your store or CRM, then add them to the file in a dedicated column. getinside does not generate them. Create them before sending the file.

::: tip Codes in bulk
Many e-commerce tools generate codes in bulk. Check your tool's help.
:::

### The QR code landing page

The scan leads to a page dedicated to the campaign, fast and mobile-friendly. See the [post-scan landing page](/en/recruit/sponsored-mail/design-guide/best-practices#the-post-scan-landing-page).

### Approve the proof

Before printing, check the proof on real rows from your file: address, first name, promo code. Scan the QR code and check the page that opens.
