# Direct mail

A printed piece addressed to the customer by name, delivered to their mailbox. Your customers no longer open your emails, but they still open their mail.

::: tip In short
- Target your churners (email-inactive customers) with a personalized mailing and a dedicated offer.
- You supply the file, getinside cleans the addresses, prints and posts in 9 to 13 business days.
- Observed result: **Best CVR 6%** for Retain.
:::

## Format and production

- **Format:** A5 on 250 g paper, plain white envelope with no window, address printed on it. Postcard, envelope in your brand colors and other formats: on quote.
- **Personalization:** first name, campaign-specific elements, and optionally a unique promo code and QR code per recipient.
- **Production:** printing in France, FSC/PEFC-certified paper.
- **Minimum volume:** 3,500 mailings per campaign, or per set of campaigns grouped in the same week.

## Why getinside

- **Your Wallet pays for your mailings:** the revenue you earn by delivering other brands' campaigns pays for your sends. See [revenue payments](/en/monetize/payouts).
- **Clean addresses:** postal standards, duplicates, people who have moved.
- **Protected data:** once uploaded, your file can be neither viewed nor downloaded, including by getinside.

**98% deliverability**, **100% attention**: the channel requires a physical action.

## Compliance

GDPR processing based on legitimate interest, with the right to object stated on every mailing. According to the CNIL, the French data protection authority:

- People are told, when their postal address is collected, that it will be used for marketing.
- They can object simply and free of charge, at collection and on every mailing.
- Each mailing identifies the brand that is writing.
- Answer requests to exercise rights within one month.
- Keep a record of objections: the CNIL recommends at least 3 years.

Our printing partner processes your file under a processing agreement compliant with GDPR Article 28. The file is deleted after production, usually 48 hours to 15 days after upload.

::: details Reaching another brand's customers
You can also write to another brand's customers. That brand uploads its contacts to a secure server at our printing partner, with no access for getinside. You supply the creative. This route is not yet a standard offer: talk to our team.
:::
