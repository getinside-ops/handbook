## Who it is for

This mailing is for brands with an established customer base and email follow-ups in place. Part of that base no longer responds: these are your churners. They no longer open your emails and no longer buy.

## What you need

A file of your churners, with each customer's postal address. The [Prepare your file](/en/retain/direct-mail/prepare-file) guide explains how to select them and export them from your tool.

## Format and production

- **Format:** A5 on 250 g paper, in a plain white envelope with no window, address printed on it. Postcard without envelope, envelope in your brand colors and other formats: on quote.
- **Personalization:** first name, elements specific to your campaign, and optionally a unique promo code and QR code per recipient.
- **Production:** getinside prints and posts the mailing through its partners. Printing is done in France, on FSC/PEFC-certified paper.
- **Minimum volume:** 3,500 mailings per campaign, or per set of campaigns grouped in the same week. The unit cost then drops as volume grows.

## Reaching another brand's customers

You can also write to another brand's customers. That brand uploads its contacts to a secure server at our printing partner, with no access for getinside. You supply the creative. This route is not yet a standard offer: talk to our team.

- **98% deliverability:** everyone opens their mailbox, even those who no longer notice you.
- **100% attention:** a channel that requires a physical action generates attention.

## Compliance

GDPR processing based on legitimate interest, with the right to object stated on every mailing. According to the CNIL, the French data protection authority:

- People are told, when their postal address is collected, that it will be used for marketing.
- They can object simply and free of charge, at collection and on every mailing.
- Each mailing identifies the brand that is writing.
- Answer requests to exercise rights within one month.
- Keep a record of objections: the CNIL recommends keeping it for at least 3 years.

On the getinside side, our printing partner processes your file under a processing agreement compliant with GDPR Article 28. The file is deleted after production, usually 48 hours to 15 days after upload.

## Frequently asked questions

::: details Why a white envelope?
An identical envelope and finishing for every brand lets us group the week's campaigns and keep costs down. An envelope in your brand colors remains possible on quote.
:::

::: details Why a minimum of 3,500 mailings?
Printing costs drop sharply from this volume. getinside groups the week's campaigns to reach it.
:::

::: details Can I pay with my Wallet?
Yes, if your Wallet is credited by the campaigns you deliver. See [revenue payments](/en/monetize/payouts).
:::

::: details Does my file need cleaning before sending?
Do the basic checks before exporting. getinside then cleans your addresses before every print run: postal standardization, duplicates and people who have moved. See the [file guide](/en/retain/direct-mail/prepare-file).
:::

::: details Who sees my data?
Once uploaded to the secure server, your file can be neither viewed nor downloaded, including by getinside. It is deleted after production.
:::

::: details What happens to undelivered mail?
It comes back from a few days after upload, and almost all of it has returned about a month later. You receive a CSV file with the recipient's identifier and the reason for non-delivery.
:::
